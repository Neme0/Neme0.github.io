Summary PhD candidate in Civil and Environmental Engineering with over three years of interdisciplinary research experience in subsurface energy systems. Skilled in data analysis, analytical chemistry, geomechanics, fluid-rock interaction, reservoir engineering, and petrophysics. Research projects have focused on carbon capture and storage (CCS), hydrogen storage, serpentinization, geologic hydrogen, and geothermal energy.
